Welcome to my website!

I am a software engineer at Hewlett Packard Enterprise.  I mainly work on
open-source projects [Chapel](https://chapel-lang.org) and
[Arkouda](https://arkouda-www.github.io). My interests are mainly around programming
languages and frameworks that enable more users to benefit from parallel
architectures, and particularly supercomputers.

I received my B.Sc. in Computer Engineering from Bilkent University, Turkey in 2013.
Then, received my Ph.D. from The George Washington University in 2020. You
can find more details about my education and career trajectory in my [CV](cv).
